Since this is a full-stack academic project using React.js, Node.js, Express.js, and MongoDB, I’d suggest choosing a use case that is realistic enough to demonstrate CRUD, authentication, product management, cart, orders, and admin functionality, but not so large that it becomes difficult to implement.

Recommended Project:

Title: CampusCart - A Full-Stack E-Commerce Platform for College Students

Problem Statement:

College students often need to purchase products such as stationery, books, college merchandise, electronic accessories, bags, and other everyday essentials. Existing e-commerce platforms provide a large number of products, but they are not specifically designed around the needs, budget constraints, and preferences of college students.

The proposed system, CampusCart, is a full-stack e-commerce web application that provides students with a dedicated platform to browse, search, filter, and purchase student-oriented products. The application will allow users to register and log in, view product details, add products to a cart, place orders, and track their orders.

An administrator can manage products, categories, inventory, and customer orders through an admin interface. The system will use React.js for the frontend, Node.js and Express.js for the backend REST APIs, and MongoDB for storing user, product, cart, and order information.

Specific Use Case:

Use case: A college student purchasing academic and daily-use products

For example, a student needs to purchase a combination of:

* Notebooks and stationery
* College-branded merchandise
* Laptop accessories
* USB drives and cables
* Backpacks
* Books
* Other student essentials

Instead of searching through multiple platforms, the student can log in to CampusCart, search for the required products, filter them by category and price, view product details and reviews, add selected products to the cart, and place an order.

The administrator can then process the order and update its status, such as:

Pending → Confirmed → Shipped → Delivered

This gives you a very clear end-to-end full-stack workflow to demonstrate during your project evaluation.

End Users Benefitted:

End User:	Benefits:
College Students	Easily find and purchase student-oriented products from one platform
Student Buyers	Search, filter, compare prices, manage cart, and track orders
Administrators	Manage products, categories, inventory, users, and orders
